FORMULA E100

HOW TO PLAY

WHAT IS THIS GAME

Formula E100 is an endless arcade highway game. Drive as far as possible, avoid obstacles, collect sugarcane, upgrade your vehicle, and build the highest score across Easy, Hard, and Realism modes.

HOW TO PLAY

A / LEFT ARROWSteer left
D / RIGHT ARROWSteer right
S / DOWN ARROWBrake or decelerate
SPACEUse an earned jump
ESCOpen Continue / Return to Menu
RRestart after game over
MMute or unmute music

Avoid cars, rickshaws, trucks, humans, cows, and chai tapris. Most impacts end the run. Tier 2 and Tier 4 cars can survive hitting a human, but lose 20% of their current speed.

Collect sugarcane to upgrade your car. Every 10 sugarcanes unlocks the next permanent tier. Every 25 sugarcanes earns one jump. You cannot steer or collect sugarcane while airborne.

After reaching Tier 4, every additional 10 sugarcanes activates the Rafale for 3 seconds. During this period, the aircraft flies over obstacles at Mach 1, but cannot steer or collect sugarcane.

Your score increases through distance travelled, sugarcane collected, and time maintained at maximum speed.

In Realism mode, opening the gameplay menu does not pause the run.
