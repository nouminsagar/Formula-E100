FORMULA E100

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Drive as far as possible, avoid obstacles, collect sugarcane, upgrade the car and achieve the highest score.

CONTROLS

A / LEFT ARROWSteer left
D / RIGHT ARROWSteer right
S / DOWN ARROWBrake or decelerate
SPACEUse one earned jump charge
MMute or unmute music
RRestart after game over
ESCOpen gameplay menu or return where supported

DIFFICULTIES

EASY Four-lane road with same-direction traffic.

HARD Four-lane tarmac with two same-direction lanes and two oncoming lanes.

REALISM Narrow two-lane tarmac with drivable dirt roads on both sides. Dirt reduces speed and makes steering unstable. Dirt-road traffic may travel in either direction.

OBSTACLES

Avoid cars, rickshaws, trucks, humans, cows and chai tapris. Touching most gameplay obstacles causes immediate game over.

TIER 2 AND TIER 4 Hitting a human does not end the run, but your current speed drops by 20%.

SUGARCANE

Collect sugarcane to upgrade the car.

ALL DIFFICULTIES Tier 2: 10 sugarcanes, Tier 3: 20 sugarcanes, Tier 4: 30 sugarcanes

SPEED TIERS

Tier 1: 80 km/h

Tier 2: 100 km/h

Tier 3: 150 km/h

Tier 4: 250 km/h

JUMP

Every 25 sugarcanes earns one jump. Press SPACE during gameplay to jump over traffic, humans, cows and chai tapris. You cannot steer or collect sugarcane while airborne.

PAUSE MENU

ESC opens Continue / Return to menu. In Realism, the run continues behind the menu.

TIER 5

Collect 40 sugarcanes to activate the Rafale for five seconds. It flies at Mach 1, disables steering, flies over obstacles, cannot collect sugarcane, and scores at the Tier 1 rate.

AUDIO

Gameplay music loops continuously during each run.

M Mute or unmute music.

SCORING

Score increases through distance travelled, sugarcane collected and time maintained at maximum tier speed.
